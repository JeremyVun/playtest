# Study archive

Completed studies move here when their plans and operator material are no
longer current. Each archive has a short summary and retains the evidence needed
to understand the result.

Archived suites record the repository paths and instrument pins used when they
ran. They are evidence, not supported examples: use the recorded freeze commit
or a matching worktree to reproduce them rather than running them against the
current harness unchanged.
